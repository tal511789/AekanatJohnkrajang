Place a furniture piece or complete room here as room.glb, then set ROOM_MODEL_URL in ../studio.js.
Set MODEL_MODE to 'object' for a furniture piece or 'room' for a complete room.
Use MODEL_ROTATION_Y to turn an asset that faces away from the camera.
If the screen mesh is named MonitorScreen, clicking it will zoom toward it.
The supplied 3_d_room.glb is bundled and selected by default in ../studio.js.
It contains geometry but no material or texture data. Temporary colors are
assigned in the GLB loading block of ../studio.js.
